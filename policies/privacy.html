Our Commitment

At HillCraft, your trust is as valuable as every handcrafted creation made by the women artisans of Uttarakhand. We are committed to protecting your personal information and using it responsibly while providing you with a safe and enjoyable shopping experience through The Hills Still Call.

Information We Collect

When you place an order or interact with our website, we may collect your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, state, PIN code, order details and any information you voluntarily provide while contacting us or submitting memories.

Why We Collect This Information

We collect this information to process orders, provide customer support, generate receipts, manage deliveries, communicate important order updates, improve our services and maintain the security of our website.

Data Security

HillCraft takes reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect customer information from unauthorised access, misuse or disclosure. Customer data is only accessible to authorised members responsible for order management, customer support and website administration.

Third-Party Services

To provide our services, HillCraft may use trusted third-party providers for secure payments, database management, email communication, website hosting and delivery services. These providers receive only the information necessary to perform their services.

Memories & Photographs

Memories, stories and photographs submitted through The Hills Still Call are reviewed before publication. Approved submissions may appear on our website. If HillCraft wishes to use submitted content for promotional purposes outside the website, we will first request the contributor's permission.

AI-Assisted Content

Contributors may use AI tools to translate or improve the language of genuine personal experiences. However, fabricated stories or fictional experiences presented as real memories may be rejected during the review process.

Your Rights

Customers may contact HillCraft to request corrections to their personal information or ask questions regarding the information we hold. Contributors may also request the removal of their approved memories or photographs from our website. HillCraft may verify the identity of the requester before processing such requests.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

HillCraft may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect improvements to our services, legal requirements or operational changes. The latest version will always be available on this website with the updated revision date.

Contact Us

For any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact HillCraft through our official email, phone number or other contact details provided on the Contact Us page.
