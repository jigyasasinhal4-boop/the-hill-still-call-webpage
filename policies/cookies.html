What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored by your web browser that help websites remember information about your visit. They improve website functionality and make your browsing experience smoother.

How HillCraft Uses Cookies

The Hills Still Call uses essential browser storage and similar technologies to support important website features such as maintaining your preorder session, remembering temporary information during checkout and improving website performance.

Third-Party Services

Some third-party services used by HillCraft, such as payment providers, hosting services and analytics tools, may also use cookies or similar technologies according to their own privacy policies.

Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control, block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling essential cookies or browser storage may affect the proper functioning of certain features of this website.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding our use of cookies or browser storage, please contact HillCraft through our official support channels.
