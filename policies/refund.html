Refund & Cancellation Policy

Privacy Terms Refunds Shipping Contact

Last Updated: July 2026

Order Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation of their preorder before production begins. In such cases, HillCraft will issue a refund of 90% of the amount paid. The remaining 10% will be retained to cover administrative and processing costs.

Cancellation After Production Begins

Once production of a handcrafted product has started, cancellation requests cannot be accepted. As each item is created specifically for the customer, advance payments become non-refundable after production has commenced.

Refused Deliveries

If a customer refuses to accept a delivered order without a valid reason, the parcel will be returned to HillCraft. The advance payment will not be refunded, as production, packaging and shipping costs have already been incurred.

Damaged Products

If a product is received in a damaged condition, customers may contact HillCraft within 15 days of delivery. Customers may choose either a refund for the damaged product or a replacement. For replacements, the damaged product must first be returned to HillCraft. If the customer upgrades to a higher-priced product, they will only be required to pay the price difference, and HillCraft may provide a 10% discount on the upgraded replacement.

Incorrect Product Delivered

If HillCraft delivers an incorrect product due to our error, customers may either keep the product or return it for a replacement with the correct item. HillCraft will bear the return shipping and replacement shipping costs in such cases.

Handmade Variations

Every HillCraft product is handcrafted. Minor variations in colour, embroidery placement, texture or natural materials are part of the handmade process and make each product unique. Such variations are not considered defects and are not eligible for refunds. Customers may request a replacement under the standard replacement policy if they wish to exchange the item.

Return Eligibility

Refund or replacement requests must be made within 15 calendar days from the date of delivery. Returned products must be unused, unwashed, unaltered and in their original condition. Used or intentionally damaged products are not eligible for refunds or replacements.

Lost or Undelivered Orders

If a customer reports that an order has not been received after being marked as delivered, HillCraft will conduct a fair investigation. Evidence may include courier confirmation, delivery photographs, GPS records, OTP or signature confirmation and any information provided by the customer. If the investigation determines that the courier is responsible, HillCraft will provide an appropriate resolution and pursue the matter with the delivery partner. If sufficient evidence confirms successful delivery, the refund request may be declined.

Production & Delivery Delays

As every HillCraft product is handcrafted, production or delivery may occasionally be delayed due to severe weather, natural disasters, transport disruptions or other circumstances beyond our reasonable control. In such situations, customers will be informed, but compensation or refunds will not be provided solely because of the delay. If a delay occurs due to an error on HillCraft's part, we will review the situation and provide an appropriate resolution where necessary.

How to Request a Refund or Replacement

Customers should contact HillCraft through our official customer support channels with their Order ID, a description of the issue and any supporting photographs or information. HillCraft will review every request individually and communicate the outcome as soon as reasonably possible.

Need Help?

If you have any questions regarding refunds, cancellations or replacements, please contact HillCraft using the official contact information provided on our Contact Us page.
