Handcrafted Production

Every HillCraft product is individually handcrafted by women artisans of Uttarakhand. Because of this, orders require production time before they are dispatched. Estimated production and delivery timelines may vary depending on the product and order volume.

Shipping Charges

Shipping charges are calculated separately before dispatch and are communicated to the customer before the remaining payment is collected. Shipping costs may vary depending on the delivery location and courier partner.

Order Verification

Before dispatching an order, HillCraft makes reasonable efforts to verify customer information and delivery details. If additional clarification is required, our team may contact the customer before shipping the order.

Delivery Delays

Unexpected delays caused by weather conditions, natural disasters, transportation disruptions, government restrictions or other circumstances beyond HillCraft's reasonable control may affect delivery times. While we will keep customers informed, compensation or refunds will not be provided for delays resulting from such circumstances.

Receiving Your Order

Customers are requested to inspect their package upon delivery. If any visible damage is noticed, customers should contact HillCraft as soon as possible and preferably within 15 days along with supporting photographs.

Contact

For any shipping-related questions, customers may contact HillCraft through our official customer support channels.
